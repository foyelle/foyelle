# Code of conduct

Foyelle follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
in its issues, discussions, pull requests and anywhere people talk about the project in its name.

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, welcome people whatever their background or
experience, and criticise ideas, not people. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not
accepted.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, report it to the maintainers privately on GitHub: use
"Report content" on the comment, issue or pull request and choose to report it to the
repository's maintainers. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainers decide what to do, from a
warning to a ban, as the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
