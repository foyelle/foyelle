Foyelle

a computer you talk to

An operating system with one full-screen canvas, no windows and no apps. You chat with Foyelle and its bots, by typing or talking, and what you need appears as cards on the same canvas.

At an early stage and still experimental. Things break, change or disappear without notice. Don't use it with data or accounts you depend on.

Yours to control

Use a local model or an online one, and connect the services you choose. Every tool call goes through a policy you can read, anything that sends, deletes or changes the computer asks first, and every system change can be rolled back.

Foyelle is built on Hermes Agent, NixOS and MCP.
